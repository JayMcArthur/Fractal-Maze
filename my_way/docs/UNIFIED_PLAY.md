Balanced copy search computes finite reachability between exact initial and finish stacks without enumerating copy stacks. It returns a shortest transition witness for the declared model, with a cap on expanded playback size. Failure concerns that model and finite rule; it does not settle a WIP drawing or an infinite derivation. Search runs in a cancelable worker.
