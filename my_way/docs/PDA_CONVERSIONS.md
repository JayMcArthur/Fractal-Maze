## Infinite and Cantor evidence

The three Siggy SVGs retain source geometry and explicit movement rules. The proof notebook starts empty. Users build witnesses and check their own claims; it does not reveal a source proof automatically. Infinite hops support independent left/right setup trails followed by witnesses reaching the same nonempty repeating copy word on both sides. A diagram compares their repeated addresses; different fixed suffixes can converge to the same infinite address. Multiple checked proofs may establish the same connection, and checked connections can compose into a replayable proof solution. Cantor hypotheses can only be used at a strictly deeper address, with a complete witness for each declared companion. Saved lemmas are rechecked after topology or rule changes.

Windischer’s playable conversion remains unfinished and in scope; Jay deferred v6 image mapping. Its three source views remain grouped in a WIP conversion workspace without endpoints or transitions. Infinite recursion does not by itself authorize passing through the center, and no central proof edge is invented.
